Privacy Policy

Applies to Bulk Delete ChatGPT Chats. Last updated: 2026-11-04.

We collect nothing. The extension has no server of its own, no analytics, no crash reporting and no remote logging.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (delay between actions, auto-archive days, language).
  • Your Pro status and license key, if you bought Pro.
  • The progress of an interrupted run, so it can resume.
  • A counter of successful runs, used only to decide when to ask for a review once.
  • On the free plan, today's usage count (a date and a number). A copy is kept in chatgpt.com's local storage in your browser so the daily limit survives a reinstall.

These are stored with Chrome's extension storage (synced through your own Chrome account if you turned sync on) and never sent to us.

What the extension reads

On chatgpt.com it reads conversation titles and links in the sidebar and on the Archived chats page, to show checkboxes and apply your filters. It never reads message contents and never transmits titles or links.

Permissions

Only storage, plus a content script on chatgpt.com. No other site access.

Payments

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy collects your email and payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card. The extension contacts Lemon Squeezy's license API only to check your license key.

Debug information

If a run fails you can press “Copy debug info”. It goes to your clipboard only. It is never uploaded.
