Privacy Policy

Applies to this website and the Bits Sweep extension. Last updated: 2026-11-04.

The extension collects nothing. It has no server of its own, no analytics, no crash reporting and no remote logging. The only personal data we ever receive is what you choose to give us when you buy Pro or email us, described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (delay between actions, auto-archive days, language).
  • Your Pro status and license key, if you bought Pro.
  • The progress of an interrupted run, so it can resume.
  • A counter of successful runs, used only to decide when to ask for a review once.
  • On the free plan, today's usage count (a date and a number). A copy is kept in chatgpt.com's local storage in your browser so the daily limit survives a reinstall.

These are stored with Chrome's extension storage (synced through your own Chrome account if you turned sync on) and never sent to us.

What the extension reads

In your own signed-in ChatGPT tab, the extension asks chatgpt.com, with the same requests the ChatGPT website makes, for the list of your conversations: id, title, created and last-updated time, and whether each is archived. It shows them in its panel and applies your filters. When you confirm an action it asks chatgpt.com to archive, unarchive or delete exactly the conversations you selected. It never reads message contents. The list stays in that tab's memory and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Permissions

Only storage, plus a content script on chatgpt.com. Network requests go only to chatgpt.com, from your own tab, and to Lemon Squeezy's license API.

Payments

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy collects your email and payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card. Lemon Squeezy shares your name, email address and order details with us, and we use them only for support, refunds and license problems. The extension contacts Lemon Squeezy's license API only to check your license key.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to reply to you. Mail is forwarded to our mailbox through Cloudflare Email Routing.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Cloudflare for its domain, which may process your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you, correct or delete the order details and emails we hold about you. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored in your browser.

Debug information

If a run fails you can press “Copy debug info”. It goes to your clipboard only. It is never uploaded.
